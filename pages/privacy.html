Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Memorii Events ("we", "us") is a photo booth business operated as a sole proprietorship in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send a booking or quote request, we collect your full name, your phone number or email address, the type of event, the event date, your budget, and any extra details you choose to add.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, prepare pricing and availability, and, if you book with us, arrange your event. We do not sell your personal information.

How requests are delivered

Our forms are processed by Formspree, a form-handling service, which forwards each request to our business email inbox at memoriieventsbooking@gmail.com.

Keeping your information

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and to manage bookings.

Fonts

This website loads the Montserrat font from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical information such as your IP address when the font is requested.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us. Email memoriieventsbooking@gmail.com.
