Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

Introduction

Memorii Events values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect it.

Types of Information Collected

We may collect personal information from you when you use our website or book our photo booth services, such as your name, email address, phone number, and event details. Our website host and font provider may also receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when you visit.

Use of Information

We may use your personal information to provide our photo booth services, communicate with you about your booking, and send you promotional materials about our services. We may also use it to improve our website and services, and for other purposes consistent with the nature of our business.

Sharing of Information

We may share your personal information with third-party vendors, contractors, or service providers who help us provide our services, such as payment processors and email service providers. Our website forms are processed by Formspree, which sends each request to our inbox at memoriieventsbooking@gmail.com. We may also share your information if required by law or to protect our legal rights or property.

Fonts

This website loads the Montserrat font from Google Fonts. Google may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when the font is requested.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to access and correct your personal information, and to ask us to delete it. To do so, email us at memoriieventsbooking@gmail.com.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to change or update this privacy policy at any time without notice. Your continued use of our website or services after any change means you accept the revised policy.

By using our website or services, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to this privacy policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at memoriieventsbooking@gmail.com.
