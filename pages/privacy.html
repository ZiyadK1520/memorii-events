Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Memorii Events is a photo booth business operated as a sole proprietorship in Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send a booking or quote request, we collect your full name, your phone number or email address, the type of event, the event date, your budget, and any extra details you choose to add.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, share pricing and availability, and arrange your event if you book with us. We don’t sell your personal information.

How requests reach us

Our forms are processed by Formspree, a form-handling service, which sends each request to our business inbox at memoriieventsbooking@gmail.com.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond to you and manage bookings.

Fonts

This website loads the Montserrat font from Google Fonts. Google may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address, when the font is requested.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information you’ve sent us. Email memoriieventsbooking@gmail.com.
